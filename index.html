How a Dubai sale works

Four steps from first call to new owner

Resale property in Dubai follows the same path each time. We guide buyers and sellers through every step.

  1. Tell us the brief

    Area, budget or asking price, and when you want to move. A few lines are enough to start.

  2. Viewings and listing

    Buyers see units that match. Sellers get a price based on recent sales and a listing that reaches buyers.

  3. Agree and sign Form F

    Once the price is agreed, both sides sign Form F, the standard memorandum of understanding for a resale.

  4. NOC and transfer

    The developer issues a no-objection certificate where needed, then ownership transfers at a Dubai Land Department trustee office.

Renting follows a shorter path: shortlist, viewing, contract, then Ejari registration.
